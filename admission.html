Eligibility and admission process

Admission eligibility, entrance requirements, available courses, dates, and fees may change by program and academic session. Please check the official MIT website and relevant admission authorities for the latest verified information.

  1. Explore programs

    Review the current programs and their eligibility criteria.

  2. Check official admission guidance

    Confirm the applicable entrance exam, counselling process, and important dates.

  3. Prepare documents

    Keep required academic records and identity documents ready as specified by the official process.

  4. Submit your application

    Follow the official application and fee-payment instructions.

  5. Track updates

    Monitor official notices for counselling, verification, and admission status.

Important: This student project does not collect applications or payments. Always use the official institute/admission portal for authentic instructions.
