About the institute

Moradabad Institute of Technology (MIT), located in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh, was established in 1996 by Moradabad Educational Trust. The institute is affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU).

MIT's educational focus includes academic learning, practical knowledge, innovation, and professional development. The institute aims to help students strengthen their abilities and prepare for evolving career opportunities.

Our vision

To develop competent, innovative, and ethical professionals who contribute to technological advancement, sustainable development, and the progress of society.

Our mission

  • Provide quality education through effective teaching and practical learning.
  • Encourage innovation, research, creativity, and problem-solving.
  • Develop leadership, teamwork, communication, and professional skills.
  • Promote ethical values, social responsibility, and lifelong learning.
  • Prepare students for employment, entrepreneurship, and higher education.
